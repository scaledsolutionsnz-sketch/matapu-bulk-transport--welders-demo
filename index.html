Our truck, our load

Based at Matapu, working the whole district

Matapu Bulk Transport & Welders runs truck and trailer units out of Lower Duthie Road, Kaponga. The work is bulk cartage for farms across South Taranaki: fertiliser, stock feed, blend mixes, maize and Osflo, plus machinery shifted when it needs to move.

The welding side backs it up. Steel repairs, fabrication and rust work get done in the workshop, so the same crew that carts your load can fix the gear it lands in.

  • Locally owned and operated at Matapu, Kaponga
  • Truck and trailer units for full bulk loads
  • Cartage and welding from the one yard
